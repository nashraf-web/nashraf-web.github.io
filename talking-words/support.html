Contact us

Email contact@cloudemythos.org for any question, problem, or purchase issue. Tell us what happened, which device you use, and what you see on screen — we answer every message.

Restore a purchase

If a subscription or the lifetime unlock ever looks missing, open the Shop (or the Parent area) in the app and tap Restore Purchases. The App Store or Google Play checks your purchase and unlocks it again — a purchase is never lost on update.

Parent PIN

Every purchase sits behind a parent PIN, so a child can never buy anything alone. You create any 4-digit PIN the first time you open the parent area. If you forget it, use the recovery question in the parent area to set a new one.

Cancel a subscription

Cancel anytime in Settings on your device (App Store subscriptions on iPhone, Google Play subscriptions on Android). You keep access until the paid period ends — nothing is charged after that.

About the app

  • 140 English words for kids ages 3–7, across 7 themes. Animals is free forever.
  • No ads, no account, no tracking. Learning works fully offline.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it remembers on your device.
