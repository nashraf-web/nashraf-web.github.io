Talking Words — Privacy Policy

App: Talking Words (package com.ouzztech.talking_words)
Developer: OuzzTech
Last updated: November 3, 2026

The short version

The app collects nothing. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing about your child ever leaves the device.

What we collect

Nothing. Talking Words does not collect, store, or share any personal information — from children or from parents.

  • No account or sign-up. There is no email, no name, no password, no profile.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no tracking code.
  • No ads. The app shows no advertisements of any kind.
  • No location. The app never asks for or uses location.

What stays on the device

Everything the app remembers lives only on your device, and only for the app to work:

  • Learning progress (words learned, quiz scores, streaks, stickers, favorites).
  • Parent settings (screen-time limit, PIN, sound preferences, purchase state cache).
  • Nothing is uploaded, synced, or backed up to any server. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Speech and sound

  • Words are spoken using your device's built-in text-to-speech. Speaking practice uses your device's built-in speech recognition. Both run through your device's operating system; no recordings are sent anywhere by the app, and no recordings are stored.
  • Learning in the app works fully offline. The release version requests internet access only to verify purchases with the store (Google Play Billing and RevenueCat) — no other network use, and nothing else leaves the device.

Purchases

  • Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play (and RevenueCat, which reads your store purchase status to unlock content). We never see or store your payment details, and we never see your store account information.
  • Subscription status is checked with the store so your purchases survive app updates. See "Restore Purchases" in the app's Shop or Parent area if anything ever looks missing.
  • Purchases are protected by a parent PIN inside the app, so a child cannot buy anything alone.

Children

Talking Words is designed for children learning English, and it is built to be safe for them: no personal information is collected from children, no chat, no social features, no external links in any child-facing screen, and no ads. The only links (this policy, support contact) are inside the parent area, behind the parent PIN.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date. The app itself will not change how it handles information without updating this page first.
