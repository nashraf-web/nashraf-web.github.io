Questions parents actually ask.

Straight answers, no fine print. Anything else — write to us; a human answers.

What is TidyTots?

TidyTots is a chore chart and allowance app for kids ages 4–17, led by Ash the wise wolf. Parents set chores, children finish them and add a photo as proof, parents approve with one tap, and coins land in Spend, Save and Share jars. Every coin is written into an auditable ledger.

How much does TidyTots cost?

7 free days first, with no card and nothing to cancel. After that, Family Premium covers every child and every core feature — see the current price in the app (Parent Zone) or on the Pricing page, as prices differ slightly between the App Store and Google Play.

Will I be charged automatically after the 7 free days?

No. No card is taken and the 7 days are granted by the app itself, so nothing can auto-convert into a charge. When the 7 free days end, the app simply stops adding new activity until a parent chooses a plan.

Can my child buy things in the app?

No. Real-money purchases exist only behind the parent PIN. In Kid Mode a child can only spend coins they earned, and even that asks for the parent PIN first.

Are there ads in TidyTots?

No. There are zero ads for kids, always — no ads in Kid Mode, no ads in the Parent Zone, and no advertising SDK or analytics SDK anywhere in the app.

What ages is TidyTots for?

Kids ages 4–17. Picture mode for ages 4–9 uses big picture chore cards and one giant done button; money mode for ages 10–17 uses real amounts, a kid-readable ledger, and savings goals. Chore packs are banded 4–6, 7–9, 10–12 and 13–17, all editable.

Does TidyTots work offline?

Yes, fully offline. Family data is encrypted on the device, and the parent and child views stay in sync on that same device automatically, without any account and without the internet.

What happens to our data if we stop paying?

It stays viewable, exportable and deletable forever, even if you never pay. You can delete a child's profile or wipe all family data inside the app at any time.

Does TidyTots work for two-home and custody families?

Yes. Custody mode is built in: chore generation, reminders and allowance follow your child's calendar — alternating weeks, 2-2-3, weekends, or your own pattern — and a gentle pause mode covers holidays and hard weeks without guilt. TidyTots works on one device per family in this version; multi-device sync is planned for a later version.

Who is Ash?

Ash is a wise wolf mentor who shares short, pre-written wisdom cards about effort, saving, kindness and staying safe. Every card is parent-approved before a child can see it, parents can preview every card and switch any theme off, and Ash never chats live with children.

What if I forget my parent PIN?

Use your device biometrics to open the Parent Zone and set a new PIN — no old PIN needed. If you set a one-time recovery code at setup, that works too. Never enroll a child's fingerprint or face in device biometrics if that child should not be able to open the Parent Zone.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

On iPhone: cancel anytime in Settings → your name → Subscriptions; Family Premium stays active until the end of your current period. Refunds follow Apple's policies — request them through Apple's report-a-problem page. On Android: cancel anytime in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions); refunds follow Google Play's policies — request them in Google Play. If either store declines a refund, write to us and we will review your case in good faith.

How do I delete our data?

Inside the app, in the Parent Zone: delete a child's profile or wipe all family data. There is no server copy of your family's data to delete — everything deletable lives in the app. Questions: write to the support address below.
