Privacy Policy — Live Football: Goal Alerts

Developer: OuzzTech  |  Effective date: October 25, 2026  |  Contact: contact@cloudemythos.org

1. The short version

Live Football works without an account. You do not sign in, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. Your favourite teams and alert settings live on your device only. The app shows football scores, tables, and goal alerts using free public data sources, and it uses three third-party services to do its job: Google AdMob (ads), RevenueCat with Google Play (ad-removal subscriptions), and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push alerts). Those services handle their own data as described below; we do not run our own analytics or tracking.

2. Data stored on your device (not sent to us)

  • Favourite teams — the team names you star, so the app can filter matches and alerts for you.
  • Alert settings — whether goal alerts are on, and your quiet-hours window.
  • Cached scores and crests — recent match data and team logos, so the app loads fast and works briefly offline.

All of this stays in the app's private storage on your phone. Deleting the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. We have no server copy of it and no way to read it.

4. What we do NOT collect

  • No precise location, no contacts, no photos, no microphone or camera access.
  • No analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK: we do not measure how you use the app.
  • No advertising data is collected by us directly; advertising data is handled by Google AdMob as described above.

5. Children's privacy

Live Football is a general-audience sports scores app. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from children: there is no account, no chat, and no user-generated content. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service listed above, contact that provider or us and we will help point you to the right deletion route.

6. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data: kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Subscriptions: managed in Google Play; cancelling is done in your Play Store subscriptions, not in the app.
  • Third-party data: advertising, purchase, and push data are held by Google, RevenueCat, and Firebase under their own policies; use their settings and opt-outs linked above to manage them.

7. Security

All network requests in the app use HTTPS. Because there is no account and no server copy of your favourites or settings, there is no account database that could leak them.
