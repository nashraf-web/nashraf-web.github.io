1. Who may use TidyTots

TidyTots is a tool for parents and guardians. The person who set up the app on the device must be an adult (18 or older). TidyTots has no account and no sign-in; "account holder" in these terms means that parent. Only the account holder sets up the family, sets the parent PIN, accepts these terms, and makes purchases. Children use TidyTots only as profiles inside the parent's app, under the parent's supervision. By setting up a family you confirm you are the parent or legal guardian of the children you add, or have their parent's or guardian's permission.

2. What TidyTots is — and is not

  • TidyTots tracks chores, approvals, allowance, and savings goals for your family. Balances in the app are records, not money. The app never holds, moves, or transfers real funds, and it never connects to a bank account.
  • When an app balance is "paid out," that means you have marked it settled — any real money changes hands between you and your child, offline, in your own home. If your family uses points mode, points are purely symbolic: they have no cash value, are not redeemable for anything, and any real-world equivalent is one you set and honor yourself.
  • TidyTots is not a childcare, supervision, or safety service. It does not watch, locate, or monitor your children.

6. Safety guidance — please read

TidyTots' suggested chores, age bands, and the advice given by Ash (the app's mentor character) follow recognized pediatric guidance (including the American Academy of Pediatrics and the American Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry) and published research. They are suggestions only. You know your child: you choose which tasks are appropriate, you adapt them to your home, and you supervise every task your child performs. Some chores carry a safety note in the app (for example, where cleaning products are involved) — read it before assigning the chore, and follow the in-app rule we teach every child: ask a grown-up first. Responsibility for any accident or injury during a chore rests with parental supervision, not with the app. Nothing in TidyTots is medical, psychological, or professional advice.
